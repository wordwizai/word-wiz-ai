NAME: Henny Penny
PLOT DESC: An acorn falls on Henny Penny's head and she decides the sky is falling. She rushes off to tell the king and picks up a line of birds who believe her, until sly Foxy Loxy offers them a shortcut to his den. The birds escape just in time. The story teaches about not panicking and not believing everything you hear.
STORY CONTENT:
One day Henny Penny was pecking at corn in the farmyard.
An acorn fell from a tree and hit her on the head.
Henny Penny jumped and flapped her wings.
She was sure that the sky was falling.
She decided she must run and tell the king.
On the way, she met Cocky Locky.
She told him the sky was falling, and he wanted to come too.
So Henny Penny and Cocky Locky hurried down the road.
Soon they met Ducky Lucky.
They told her the sky was falling, and she wanted to come too.
Then they met Goosey Loosey, who was scared and joined them right away.
Next they met Turkey Lurkey.
Now there were five birds rushing down the road to see the king.
They flapped and squawked the whole way.
Then they met Foxy Loxy.
Foxy Loxy asked where they were going in such a hurry.
They told him the sky was falling and they had to tell the king.
Foxy Loxy smiled a sly smile.
He said he knew a shortcut to the king's palace.
He led them into the dark woods and up to a hole in a hill.
He said the palace was just inside.
Cocky Locky looked into the hole and saw a pile of feathers and bones.
It was not the palace at all. It was the fox's den!
Cocky Locky crowed as loud as he could.
All the birds turned around and ran home as fast as they could go.
Foxy Loxy had no dinner that night.
Back at the farm, Henny Penny looked up at the sky.
It was blue and calm, right where it always was.
She found the acorn on the ground and felt very silly.
