NAME: Rumpelstiltskin
PLOT DESC: A miller brags that his daughter can spin straw into gold, so the king locks her in a room full of straw. A strange little man spins it for her in return for her necklace, her ring, and finally a promise to give him her first child. She can keep her baby only if she guesses his name, which she learns just in time. The story is about bragging and being careful what you promise.
STORY CONTENT:
A poor miller wanted the king to think he was important.
So he told the king that his daughter could spin straw into gold.
The king locked the girl in a room full of straw with a spinning wheel.
He told her to spin it all into gold by morning.
The girl did not know how to spin straw into gold, so she began to cry.
Then the door opened, and a strange little man walked in.
He asked what she would give him if he spun the straw for her.
She gave him her necklace.
The little man sat at the wheel, and whirr, whirr, whirr, the straw turned into gold.
The next night, the king put her in a bigger room with more straw.
The little man came again, and this time she gave him her ring.
By morning, all of that straw was gold too.
On the third night, the room was even bigger.
The king said that if she spun it all, she would become the queen.
But the girl had nothing left to give.
The little man said she must promise him her first baby when she was queen.
She was scared, so she made the promise.
The straw turned into gold, and the girl became the queen.
A year later, she had a beautiful baby boy.
She had forgotten all about her promise.
Then one day the little man came back to take the baby.
The queen cried and offered him all the gold in the kingdom.
He said no, but he gave her three days to guess his name.
If she guessed right, she could keep her baby.
She sent a messenger all over the land to collect names.
For two days she guessed every name she could think of.
The little man just laughed and said that was not his name.
On the third day, the messenger came back with a strange story.
Deep in the woods, he had seen a little man dancing around a fire.
The man was singing that nobody knew his name was Rumpelstiltskin.
When the little man came back, the queen asked if his name was Rumpelstiltskin.
The little man screamed and stamped his foot so hard that he fell right through the floor.
He was never seen again, and the queen kept her baby.
