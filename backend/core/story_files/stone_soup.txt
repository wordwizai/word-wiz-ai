NAME: Stone Soup
PLOT DESC: A hungry traveler comes to a village where nobody will share any food. He starts making soup with only a stone and water, and the curious villagers each add something until there is a big pot of soup for everyone. The story teaches that when everyone shares a little, there is plenty to go around.
STORY CONTENT:
A hungry traveler walked into a small village at dinnertime.
He knocked on doors and asked for something to eat.
But everyone said they had no food to spare.
They shut their doors and peeked out their windows.
The traveler did not get angry.
He set a big iron pot in the middle of the village square.
He filled it with water and built a fire under it.
Then he took a smooth gray stone out of his pocket and dropped it in the pot.
A little girl came out to see what he was doing.
He told her he was making stone soup.
She asked if soup made from a stone could really taste good.
He said it was delicious, but it was even better with a carrot.
The girl ran home and came back with a carrot.
The traveler dropped it in and stirred the pot.
A farmer walked over and sniffed the steam.
The traveler said stone soup was even better with a potato or two.
The farmer brought two potatoes from his cellar.
Soon more people came out of their houses.
One brought an onion, and another brought a bag of beans.
The baker brought a loaf of bread to eat with the soup.
An old woman brought salt and a handful of herbs from her garden.
The traveler kept stirring, and the soup smelled better and better.
At last he tasted it, smiled, and said it was ready.
The villagers carried out tables and bowls and spoons.
Everyone sat down together and ate until they were full.
They said it was the best soup they had ever eaten.
They could hardly believe it was made from a stone.
In the morning, the traveler gave his stone to the little girl and went on his way.
After that, the whole village made stone soup together once a year.
