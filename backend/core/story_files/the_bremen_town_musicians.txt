NAME: The Bremen Town Musicians
PLOT DESC: A donkey, a dog, a cat, and a rooster are all too old to be wanted by their owners, so they set off to become musicians in the town of Bremen. On the way they find a house full of robbers, scare them off with their terrible singing, and decide to stay. The story is about friendship and finding a new home when you feel unwanted.
STORY CONTENT:
A donkey had carried heavy sacks for his master for many years.
Now he was old and tired, and his master did not want him anymore.
So the donkey ran away and headed for the town of Bremen.
He wanted to be a musician there.
On the road, he met an old dog lying in the dust.
The dog was too slow to hunt, and his master did not want him either.
The donkey said the dog could come to Bremen and play the drum.
A little later, they met a cat with a sad face.
She was too old to catch mice, so her owner wanted to get rid of her.
They told her she could sing with them in Bremen.
Then they came to a farm where a rooster was crowing with all his might.
The farmer's wife was planning to cook him for Sunday dinner.
The donkey told him to come along, because he had a very good voice.
Now there were four friends walking to Bremen.
But the town was far away, and night came.
They stopped in the forest to sleep, and the rooster flew to the top of a tree.
From up there, he saw a light shining through the trees.
They walked toward it and found a little house.
The donkey peeked in the window.
He saw a gang of robbers sitting at a table full of food.
The animals made a plan to scare the robbers away.
The donkey put his front hooves on the window sill.
The dog jumped on the donkey's back, and the cat climbed on the dog.
The rooster flew up and sat on the cat's head.
Then they all began to sing at once.
The donkey brayed, the dog barked, the cat yowled, and the rooster crowed.
They crashed through the window with a terrible noise.
The robbers thought a monster was coming and ran off into the woods.
The four friends sat down and ate the whole feast.
Late that night, one robber crept back to check on the house.
The cat scratched him, the dog nipped his leg, and the donkey gave him a kick.
The robber ran back and told the others that a monster lived in the house now.
The robbers never came back.
The four friends liked the little house so much that they never went to Bremen at all.
