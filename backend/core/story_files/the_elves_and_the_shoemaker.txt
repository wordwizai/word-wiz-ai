NAME: The Elves and the Shoemaker
PLOT DESC: A poor shoemaker has only enough leather left for one pair of shoes. Every night, two tiny elves secretly finish his work, and soon he and his wife have plenty. To thank them, the couple makes the elves little clothes and boots. The story teaches about kindness and saying thank you.
STORY CONTENT:
There was once a shoemaker who worked very hard.
But he and his wife were very poor.
One night he had only enough leather left for one pair of shoes.
He cut out the pieces and left them on his workbench.
He planned to sew them in the morning, and then he went to bed.
When he came downstairs the next day, he could not believe his eyes.
A pair of shoes sat on the bench, already finished.
Every stitch was small and neat and perfect.
A man came into the shop and tried on the shoes.
He liked them so much that he paid twice the usual price.
With that money, the shoemaker bought enough leather for two more pairs.
He cut out the pieces that night and left them on the bench.
In the morning, two new pairs of shoes were waiting for him.
This happened night after night.
Soon people came from all over town to buy his shoes.
The shoemaker and his wife were not poor anymore.
One winter night, his wife said they should find out who was helping them.
So they hid behind a curtain and watched.
At midnight, two tiny elves climbed in through the window.
They had no shoes, and their clothes were old rags.
The elves picked up the tools and began to sew and hammer very fast.
They did not stop until every pair of shoes was done.
Then they ran out the window before the sun came up.
The wife said the poor elves must be cold.
She sewed them two little shirts, two pairs of pants, and two warm coats.
The shoemaker made them two tiny pairs of boots.
They left the gifts on the workbench instead of leather.
That night, the elves found the clothes and clapped their hands.
They put them on and danced all around the shop.
Then they skipped out the window and never came back.
But the shoemaker's shop did well for the rest of his life.
