NAME: The Enormous Turnip
PLOT DESC: An old man grows a turnip so big that he cannot pull it out of the ground. He calls his wife, then his granddaughter, the dog, the cat, and finally a tiny mouse, and when they all pull together the turnip pops out. The story teaches that everyone's help counts, even the smallest.
STORY CONTENT:
An old man planted a turnip seed in his garden.
He watered it every day and told it to grow big and sweet.
The turnip grew and grew.
It grew bigger than a pumpkin, and then bigger than a wheelbarrow.
One day the old man decided it was time to pull it up.
He grabbed the leaves and pulled as hard as he could.
He pulled and pulled, but the turnip did not move.
So he called his wife to help.
The old woman pulled the old man, and the old man pulled the turnip.
They pulled and pulled, but the turnip still did not come up.
The old woman called their granddaughter to help.
The girl pulled the old woman, the old woman pulled the old man, and the old man pulled the turnip.
They pulled and pulled, but the turnip stayed stuck.
The girl called the dog to help.
The dog grabbed the girl's skirt in his teeth and pulled too.
But the turnip still would not budge.
The dog barked for the cat to come and help.
The cat held on to the dog's tail, and everyone pulled together.
They pulled until their faces turned red, but the turnip stayed in the ground.
Then a tiny mouse came out of the grass.
The mouse asked if she could help.
The cat laughed, because the mouse was so small.
But the old man said that every little bit helps.
So the mouse held on to the cat's tail.
The mouse pulled the cat, the cat pulled the dog, and the dog pulled the girl.
The girl pulled the old woman, the old woman pulled the old man, and the old man pulled the turnip.
With one big pull, the turnip popped out of the ground.
Everyone tumbled over backward into a heap.
That night, they made a giant pot of turnip soup.
And the mouse got the very first bowl.
