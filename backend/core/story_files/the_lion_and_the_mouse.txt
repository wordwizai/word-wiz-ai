NAME: The Lion and the Mouse
PLOT DESC: A lion catches a mouse and lets him go after the mouse promises to help him someday. The lion laughs at the idea, but later the mouse chews through a hunter's net and sets him free. The story teaches that small friends can be a big help.
STORY CONTENT:
A big lion was sleeping in the warm sun.
A little mouse ran right across his paw.
The lion woke up and caught the mouse by the tail.
He opened his huge mouth to eat him.
The mouse squeaked and begged the lion to let him go.
He promised that he would help the lion someday.
The lion laughed so hard that his whiskers shook.
He did not think a tiny mouse could ever help a lion.
But he was not very hungry, so he let the mouse go.
The mouse thanked him and ran home to his hole.
A few days later, hunters came into the forest.
They tied a big rope net between two trees.
The lion walked by and did not see the net.
The net dropped on top of him and pulled tight.
The lion rolled and kicked, but he could not get out.
The more he pulled, the tighter the ropes got.
He let out a roar so loud that it shook the trees.
The little mouse heard the roar from far away.
He knew that sound, and he ran as fast as he could.
He found the lion tangled up in the net.
The mouse told the lion to hold still.
Then he started chewing the thick rope with his sharp little teeth.
He chewed and chewed until one rope snapped.
Then he chewed through another one, and another.
Soon there was a hole big enough for the lion to crawl through.
The lion climbed out just before the hunters came back.
He looked down at the tiny mouse and smiled.
From that day on, the lion and the mouse were best friends.
