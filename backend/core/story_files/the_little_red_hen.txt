NAME: The Little Red Hen
PLOT DESC: A hen finds some wheat and asks the cat, the dog, and the duck to help her plant it, cut it, and bake it into bread. They say no every time, so she does all the work herself. When the bread is ready they all want some, and she eats it without them. The story teaches that the people who help with the work get to share what it makes.
STORY CONTENT:
A little red hen lived on a farm with a cat, a dog, and a duck.
One morning she found a few grains of wheat in the yard.
"Who will help me plant this wheat?" asked the little red hen.
"Not I," said the cat. "Not I," said the dog. "Not I," said the duck.
"Then I will plant it myself," said the little red hen, and she did.
The sun shone and the rain fell, and the wheat grew tall and gold.
"Who will help me cut this wheat?" asked the little red hen.
"Not I," said the cat. "Not I," said the dog. "Not I," said the duck.
So the little red hen cut all the wheat by herself.
"Who will help me carry this wheat to the mill?" she asked.
Nobody wanted to carry the heavy sack.
So she dragged it down the long road to the mill all by herself.
The miller ground the wheat into soft white flour.
"Who will help me bake some bread?" asked the little red hen.
The cat was napping, the dog was digging, and the duck was splashing in the pond.
"Not I," they all said.
"Then I will bake it myself," said the little red hen.
She mixed the flour with water and a pinch of salt.
She put the dough in the hot oven and waited.
Soon a warm smell drifted across the whole farm.
The cat, the dog, and the duck came running to the kitchen door.
"Who will help me eat this bread?" asked the little red hen.
"I will!" said the cat. "I will!" said the dog. "I will!" said the duck.
"You did not help me plant it or cut it or carry it or bake it," said the little red hen.
"So I will eat it myself," she said, and she did.
She ate every last crumb.
The next spring, the little red hen found more wheat.
This time, the cat, the dog, and the duck all helped.
