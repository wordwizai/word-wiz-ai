NAME: The Magic Porridge Pot
PLOT DESC: A poor girl is given a magic pot that cooks porridge when she says "Cook, little pot, cook" and stops when she says "Stop, little pot, stop." One day her mother forgets the stopping words, and porridge floods the whole town until the girl comes home. It is a funny story about remembering instructions.
STORY CONTENT:
A little girl lived with her mother at the edge of a town.
They were so poor that they had nothing left to eat.
The girl went into the woods to look for berries.
There she met an old woman who already knew about her trouble.
The old woman gave her a little black pot.
She told the girl to say "Cook, little pot, cook," and the pot would make sweet porridge.
To make it stop, she just had to say "Stop, little pot, stop."
The girl thanked her and carried the pot home.
She set it on the table and said the magic words.
The pot bubbled and filled up with warm, sweet porridge.
When it was full, the girl said "Stop, little pot, stop," and it stopped.
She and her mother ate until they were full.
After that, they were never hungry again.
One day, the girl went out to visit a friend.
Her mother felt hungry, so she said "Cook, little pot, cook."
The pot made porridge, and the mother ate a big bowlful.
Then she wanted the pot to stop, but she could not remember the words.
She said "That's enough, pot," but the pot kept cooking.
She said "Please quit, pot," but it kept on cooking.
The porridge spilled over the top and onto the table.
It ran across the floor and filled up the kitchen.
It poured out the front door and into the street.
It filled the house next door and then the house after that.
Soon the whole street was covered in porridge.
People ran out of their houses, and nobody knew how to stop it.
At last, only one house in the whole town was not full of porridge.
Just then, the little girl came walking home.
She saw the river of porridge and knew right away what had happened.
She shouted "Stop, little pot, stop!" and the pot stopped at once.
Anyone who wanted to get back into town had to eat their way home.
