NAME: The Princess and the Pea
PLOT DESC: A prince wants to marry a real princess, but he cannot tell who is real. On a stormy night a soaking wet girl knocks on the castle gate and says she is a princess, so the queen secretly puts one pea under twenty mattresses. The girl feels the pea all night, which proves she is a real princess. It is a funny story about a very sensitive princess.
STORY CONTENT:
Once there was a prince who wanted to marry a princess.
But she had to be a real princess.
He traveled all over the world to find one.
He met many princesses, but something always seemed a little wrong.
So he came home feeling sad.
One night there was a terrible storm.
Thunder boomed and rain poured down.
Someone knocked on the castle gate.
The old king went to open it and found a girl standing in the rain.
Water ran down her hair and into her shoes.
She said that she was a real princess.
The queen did not say a word, but she had a plan.
She went into the guest room and took all the blankets off the bed.
She put one tiny green pea on the bottom of the bed.
Then she piled twenty mattresses on top of the pea.
On top of the mattresses, she piled twenty fluffy quilts.
The princess had to climb a tall ladder to get into bed.
In the morning, the queen asked her how she had slept.
The princess said she had slept terribly.
There was something hard in the bed, and she had tossed and turned all night.
Now she had bruises all over.
The queen smiled at the king.
Only a real princess could feel a pea through twenty mattresses and twenty quilts.
The prince was very happy, because he had found a real princess at last.
They were married the very next week.
The pea was put in a glass case in the royal museum.
And you can still see it there, unless someone has taken it.
