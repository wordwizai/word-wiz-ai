NAME: The Tale of Peter Rabbit
PLOT DESC: Peter Rabbit's mother tells him not to go into Mr. McGregor's garden, but he goes anyway, eats too many vegetables, and gets chased all over the garden. He loses his jacket and shoes and barely escapes, then goes to bed feeling sick while his sisters have a nice supper. The story teaches about listening to your parents.
STORY CONTENT:
Once there were four little rabbits named Flopsy, Mopsy, Cotton-tail, and Peter.
They lived with their mother in a hole under a big fir tree.
One morning, their mother said they could go out and play in the fields.
But she told them never to go into Mr. McGregor's garden.
Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail went down the lane to pick blackberries.
But Peter was naughty, and he ran straight to Mr. McGregor's garden.
He squeezed under the gate.
First he ate some lettuce and some beans.
Then he ate some radishes.
After that he felt a little sick, so he went looking for some parsley.
At the end of a row of cabbages, he ran right into Mr. McGregor.
Mr. McGregor jumped up and ran after him, waving a rake.
Peter was very scared and ran all over the garden.
He lost one shoe in the cabbages and the other shoe in the potatoes.
Without his shoes, he ran on all four feet and went even faster.
Then the buttons on his new blue jacket got caught in a net.
Some friendly sparrows flew down and told him not to give up.
Peter wiggled out of his jacket just as Mr. McGregor reached for him.
He ran into the tool shed and jumped into a watering can.
It would have been a fine place to hide, but it was full of water.
Mr. McGregor looked all over the shed.
Then Peter sneezed, and Mr. McGregor came after him again.
Peter jumped out of a window and ran.
Soon he was lost, so he sat down and cried.
Then he spotted the gate far away on the other side of the garden.
He ran as fast as he could and slipped under the gate.
He did not stop running until he got home to the big fir tree.
Mr. McGregor hung Peter's little jacket and shoes on his scarecrow.
Peter felt so sick that his mother put him to bed with a cup of tea.
But Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail had bread and milk and blackberries for supper.
