NAME: The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse
PLOT DESC: A country mouse visits his cousin in town and is amazed by the fancy food, but every meal gets interrupted by people or dogs. He decides he would rather eat plain food in peace. The story teaches that a simple, safe life can be better than a fancy, scary one.
STORY CONTENT:
A country mouse lived in a little hole at the edge of a farm field.
One day his cousin from the town came to visit.
The country mouse was happy to see him.
He served his best dinner, which was barley, corn, and a few dry seeds.
The town mouse nibbled a little bit and wrinkled his nose.
He said this was not much of a dinner.
He told his cousin that in town they ate like kings.
He invited the country mouse to come and see for himself.
So the next day, the two mice set off for the town.
They walked until dark and came to a big house.
Inside was a long table covered with food left over from a party.
There was cheese, cake, jelly, and fresh bread.
The country mouse had never seen so much food.
He climbed onto the table and took a big bite of cake.
Just then, the door flew open.
People came into the room, laughing and talking.
The two mice jumped off the table and hid in a crack in the wall.
When the room was quiet again, they crept back out.
The country mouse began to nibble a piece of cheese.
Then he heard a loud bark.
Two big dogs came running into the room.
The mice raced back to the crack in the wall with their hearts pounding.
The country mouse sat there in the dark, shaking.
He told his cousin that he was going home.
He said the food in town was very fancy.
But he would rather eat plain seeds in peace than cake in fear.
He picked up his bag and ran all the way back to the country.
That night, he ate his barley and corn in his quiet little hole.
It was the best dinner he had eaten in days.
